# M9 Playfeel Round 13 — interrupted hold input recovery

## Goal
Release-candidate integration audit focused on input trust after visibility/focus transitions. No balance or feature expansion.

## Finding
Round 8 correctly terminated active hold-to-upgrade repeat timers on `visibilitychange`, `blur`, and `pagehide`, but the Round 4 control retained closure-local interaction state after that external cancellation.

A long hold sets both:

- `holding = true`
- `suppressReleaseClick = true`

Stopping only timers/pointer capture is not sufficient. If an interaction is interrupted by focus/visibility loss and no normal release click completes that pointer sequence, stale release suppression can survive into the next fresh tap. The next tap may therefore be swallowed even though it belongs to a new interaction.

## Fix
`playfeel-round4.js` now gives `stop()` an explicit hard-cancel mode:

- normal pointer completion keeps release-click suppression long enough to discard the click generated by the same completed hold;
- external cancellation (`hidden`, `blur`, `pagehide`) calls `stop(true)`;
- hard cancellation clears both `holding` and `suppressReleaseClick`, as well as timers, visual state, active-stop registration, and pointer capture.

This preserves the original protection against the extra click after a successful long hold while ensuring an interrupted gesture cannot poison the next independent tap.

## Regression guard
`tests/playfeel-round4-contract.test.js` now requires:

- hard cancellation via `stop(true)`;
- stale `holding` and `suppressReleaseClick` reset;
- `blur` and `pagehide` cancellation hooks to remain present.

The changed Round 4 script passes `node --check`, and the updated Round 4 contract passes in a local fixture reconstructed from the exact develop source files required by that contract.
